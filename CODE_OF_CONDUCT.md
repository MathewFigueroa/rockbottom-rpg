# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ROCKBOTTOM contributors build around difficult human experiences. Be direct, patient, and humane. Treat people with respect across identity, background, disability, recovery status, housing status, military experience, and technical skill.

Acceptable behavior includes constructive review, good-faith disagreement, clear boundaries, credit for others' work, and attention to the impact of our language. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, discriminatory language, mockery of lived experience, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, game-development spaces, official community spaces, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy where possible, review evidence promptly, avoid conflicts of interest, and choose a proportionate response ranging from correction to permanent removal. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1 and its enforcement principles.
