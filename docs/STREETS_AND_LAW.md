# Street markets, scheduled work and legal consequences

The **Life** panel and agent story state expose market and legal commands for Street-path characters and discharged Army veterans. Shared daily stock and posted prices live in the authoritative world snapshot. Owned lots have server-generated IDs; buyers cannot submit prices, claim another actor’s goods, resell sold lots, or trade the protected photograph. Inventory is capped at twelve lots. Stock, serials, pending jobs, evidence and cases survive PostgreSQL/file restoration.

Radios and lamps can be inspected, repaired, sold honestly, or sold with a known fault concealed. A disputed bracelet can be returned for purchase-price reimbursement or knowingly fenced. The private seed fixes inspection results. Honest repaired sales support income reliability; dishonest transactions produce specific evidence rather than immediate convictions. Restock follows monotonic game days, so reversing the admin clock cannot repeatedly refresh stock.

Daily courier demand offers a ninety-minute scheduled route. Pay arrives at completion once, never at acceptance. Reading the folded note supplies context at the cost of the recipient’s privacy and Nell’s trust; keeping it sealed leaves the worker’s account hers to volunteer.

A high HEAT value without evidence does not produce a case. Evidence can be taken to legal aid, and accumulated documented conduct may generate a summons. A summons is not a conviction. Choices include free public defense, $180 bail held in escrow, or serving thirty-six game hours. In holding, food/rest are provided and only legal-aid actions remain available. Dana can arrange defense and explain the absence to the family center.

Documented restitution plus a defender permits diversion. Otherwise a hearing—not the report, poverty or an HEAT threshold—records one upheld count. Bail is refunded once, less a $20 court charge for an upheld count; diversion returns it all. Three subsequent days of reviewed community work can seal that count. Hearing attendance effects and the original harmed buyer’s history remain. A parent must address unresolved evidence before closing the archive.

The selected radio/bracelet campaign choices now create evidence consistently with the market system. They no longer create or remove unrelated convictions before court. These are fictional, deliberately bounded civic-noir rules, not a simulation of real legal advice or procedure.
