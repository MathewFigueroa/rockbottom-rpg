# ROCKBOTTOM: Second Wind

## Register

product

## Users

Players on desktop or phone who want a grounded multiplayer role-playing game that supports both direct play and AI-agent control. The primary loop must remain readable at a glance during active play, while deeper systems such as custody, economy, moderation, content packs, and agent supervision remain understandable without prior documentation.

## Product Purpose

ROCKBOTTOM is a server-authoritative browser RPG about rebuilding a life after homelessness and addiction. Success means making consequential choices legible without moralizing, allowing a complete manual-play path, and making agent play observable, economical, and safe through decision-gated MCP tools.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, terse, humane. The game should feel worn-in and specific, with dry humor and emotional restraint. It respects difficult subject matter by showing consequences through systems and dialogue rather than lectures.

## Anti-references

- No neon cyberpunk, fantasy gloss, casino-like reward effects, or heroic military propaganda.
- No generic SaaS dashboard styling inside the game.
- No melodramatic dialogue, moralizing copy, manipulative countdowns, or predatory monetization.
- No decorative glassmorphism, gradient text, excessive cards, or gratuitous motion.

## Design Principles

1. The world leads; interface chrome stays quiet until a decision is required.
2. Consequences remain visible and traceable, never disguised as arbitrary punishment.
3. Manual, touch, agent, and spectator play share one coherent information model.
4. Difficult scenes use restraint, concrete detail, and room for the player to decide what they mean.
5. Every critical interaction works at 390 pixels wide and without precise pointer input.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for interface text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, high-contrast state cues that do not rely on color alone, text alternatives for canvas information, touch targets of at least 44 pixels, captions/transcripts for story audio, and per-player voice mute.
